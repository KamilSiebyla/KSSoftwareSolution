Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies and does not run analytics or tracking scripts of any kind. The only thing saved in your browser is a single local-storage entry recording whether you accepted or declined the cookie-consent banner, so we don't ask again on your next visit — this is not a cookie, is never sent to a server, and is not shared with anyone.

What we collect

Nothing, automatically. This site has no contact form, no analytics, and no third-party embeds. If you email us using a link on the site, that opens your own email client and sends a message directly to us — the website itself never receives, transmits, or stores that message.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics, tracking, or any cookie-based feature, this policy will be updated first and you will be asked for consent again before anything runs — declining will mean it simply doesn't load for you.
